# Fotografias do cardápio

As dez fotografias conceituais foram geradas com a ferramenta integrada de imagens para o CoffeeDev.
Não são fotografias de produtos de uma cafeteria em operação.
Os arquivos finais estão em `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/photo_<produto>.webp` e acompanham o código-fonte.
As imagens são locais, sem downloads durante o uso do aplicativo.

## Prompt compartilhado usado na geração

Use case: product-mockup. Create a single square premium photorealistic food product photograph for the CoffeeDev café ordering app. One hero product centered and fully inside the image with generous 15% breathing room, viewed from a high 65-degree overhead angle, professional commercial food photography, warm soft large-window lighting, elegant appetizing natural textures, restrained warm cream/caramel/espresso palette, solid clean continuous light honey oak tabletop background with subtle real wood grain, uncluttered scene, shallow gentle natural shadows, crisp food detail. No illustration, no vector, no 3D render look, no text, no logo, no watermark, no collage, no hands, no floating objects, no busy props. Background must extend to all four edges.

## Complemento por produto

- cappuccino: A beautiful white ceramic cup and white saucer holding a creamy cappuccino with a clearly defined large symmetrical white HEART latte art in the center of the golden crema, realistic microfoam, tiny wisp of steam.
- espresso: A small refined white porcelain espresso cup and saucer holding glossy BLACK espresso coffee with a thin natural rich golden crema ring around the edge, dark black-brown coffee center, no milk or latte art.
- latte: An elegant off-white ceramic latte cup and saucer filled with silky café latte, lovely layered white rosetta latte art in caramel espresso milk, creamy and realistic, visually distinct from a heart cappuccino.
- coldbrew: A beautiful clear short tall-sided glass filled with deep amber-black cold brew coffee and several large crystal-clear ice cubes with realistic condensation, resting directly on the warm oak tabletop, no milk.
- mocha: A refined off-white ceramic cup and saucer of café mocha, milk coffee and chocolate topped with a modest natural swirl of whipped cream and fine dark chocolate shavings, luxurious but not oversized.
- cookie: One large freshly baked artisan chocolate chunk cookie on a small matte cream ceramic plate, golden-brown irregular edges, tender center, glossy dark chocolate chunks, delicate realistic crumbs.
- croissant: One beautifully baked traditional French butter croissant on a small cream ceramic plate, deeply golden flaky crisp laminated layers, elegant crescent shape, tiny natural pastry flakes, extremely appetizing.
- paochapa: Brazilian pão na chapa: a French bread roll sliced lengthwise, both halves open face up on a small cream ceramic plate, buttery golden toasted crisp cut surfaces and fluffy white crumb, warm freshly toasted breakfast bread.
- paoqueijo: Four traditional Brazilian pão de queijo cheese bread rolls on a small cream ceramic plate, golden delicately cracked crust and pillowy shape, one opened to show airy soft cheesy interior.
- brownie: One thick premium dark chocolate brownie square on a small cream ceramic plate, shiny crackly top, moist dense fudgy cut sides and a few dark chocolate chunks, indulgent bakery product, no ice cream.

Os PNGs gerados foram codificados em WebP, qualidade 94, na resolução original. Sem recorte, redimensionamento ou retoque.

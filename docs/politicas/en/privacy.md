Updated 21 October 2026

Responsible person: Carlos Eduardo, an individual and author of this portfolio project, not a company. Contact: https://www.linkedin.com/in/carlos-eduardo-a8bb50268/ . LinkedIn may require an account. Never post personal information in public GitHub issues.

The distributed local demo stores test accounts and orders in private Android SQLite storage. Settings store language, theme, favorites, cart and retry identifiers. The local variant has no internet permission, advertising, Analytics, Crashlytics, location, camera, contacts or email sending. Fonts and concept images are bundled. The administrator account is public and can administer all test accounts and orders in that installation. Use fictional data and a test-only password; never enter confidential, financial, health or third-party data.

Name/email identify the test account; ID, role and approval status control access. Passwords are derived with PBKDF2 and random salt, not stored as plaintext. Legacy hashes are upgraded after a valid login. Session state keeps access. Orders contain items, totals, time, status and optional notes. Approval is manual, with no profiling or automated decisions having legal effects.

Where applicable, strictly necessary data supports the test service requested by the user (LGPD article 7, V). Reading terms is not blanket consent. There is no marketing, sale of data or optional tracking consent. The legal basis must be reviewed before real use; a portfolio label does not automatically exempt the project from LGPD. This demonstration is intended for adults and has no child-data flow.

Local data remains until deletion, app-data clearing or uninstall; signing out only ends the session. There is no automatic retention timer. Old preferences are cleared after migration. App backups are disabled. Android protections do not ensure confidentiality on a compromised device. Deletion is logical and does not guarantee physical erasure of flash memory. Exported files must be removed separately by their holder.

Rights may include confirmation/access, correction, anonymization/blocking/erasure of unnecessary, excessive or unlawfully processed data, portability under regulation, information on sharing and consent, withdrawal where consent applies, objection where applicable and review of applicable automated decisions. JSON export is an access copy, not a guarantee of complete regulated portability. Local visitor accounts can erase their own account/orders even while pending or blocked; the fixed demo owner can reset the installation. Other requests and correction use the form/contact without a fee. Identity verification must be proportionate, without routine ID-document collection. Simplified confirmation/access can be immediate; full access statements follow the applicable legal period, generally up to 15 days under article 19. Other rights have their own requirements.

The form is an in-memory draft, not a submission or received-case number. You may copy it and send a private message to the author. No form field is inserted into the external link. Clipboard and external-link actions are explicit. LinkedIn applies its own policy and may process data abroad. Requests received through that channel are used to handle the request, minimized and discarded after handling unless justified legal retention applies. Availability/accessibility of the external channel depends on its provider.

A separate optional cloud source variant uses Firebase Auth/Firestore; it is not the delivered local APK. If activated, Google services process account identifiers, name/email, authentication information, orders and technical metadata; the owner can see registrations/orders. Server locations, Auth processing, contracts, international-transfer safeguards, retention and server deletion must be defined and validated before real users. A Brazilian database location alone does not rule out transfers. Clearing a device does not delete server data. This policy does not approve a future deployment. Changes in purpose/services require review and prior notice.

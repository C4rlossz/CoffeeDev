Accessibility statement — 21 October 2026

Responsible person: Carlos Eduardo. We use Android guidance and WCAG 2.2 as technical references, without claiming certification or full WCAG conformance.

This revision includes explicit tested text/background pairs in light/dark themes, revised outlines, at least 48 dp custom-button targets, labeled fields and controls, named product images, native Material states, semantic headings and a single-column menu at larger font scales. Key information is not conveyed by color alone. Android font scaling is supported. Portuguese, English and Spanish are available; TalkBack voice pronunciation depends on device language settings.

Limits: static/automated checks do not replace manual TalkBack, keyboard/Switch Access, large-font and multiple-device testing. No independent evaluation with disabled users was performed. The external LinkedIn channel is outside the project’s accessibility control.

The request form can describe the screen, action, expected/observed results and optionally Android version/assistive technology. Do not provide diagnoses, health data or ID documents. The draft is not submitted automatically: copy it and send it privately through the indicated channel. No official audit seal or certificate is claimed.

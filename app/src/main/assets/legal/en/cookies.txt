Cookies and storage — 21 October 2026

This native local Android APK has no embedded browser, HTTP cookies, advertising or trackers identified in the reviewed source. No cookie-consent banner is shown because no optional cookies or tracking are implemented.

SQLite and private settings support requested functions: test accounts/orders, session, language, theme, cart, favorites and retry IDs. Signing out does not erase these records. You can erase a local visitor account, reset all demo data as owner, clear app data in Android settings or uninstall. Exported files are separate.

LinkedIn opens externally, only by your action, and applies its own cookie/privacy rules. CoffeeDev does not manage those cookies.
